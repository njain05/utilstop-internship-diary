## What is this project?

UtilStop is a multi-service booking platform operating in UAE and India. Customers use it to book Home Cleaning, Furniture Care, Home Maintenance, Logistics & Relocation, Pest Control, and Personal Care services. The mobile app already exists. My internship task is to convert it into a full web application — desktop-first, same features, same brand.
